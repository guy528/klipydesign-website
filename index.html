02 / Capabilities

Four moving parts, one assembly.

Most studios hand a drawing to a factory. We stay on the part — concept, prototype, mechanism and tooling are run by the same team, so a product reaches production with nothing lost in translation.

We start from the commercial brief, not the moodboard. Sketch cycles, orthographic layouts and 1:1 ergonomic mock-ups let us prove the proportions in the hand before any CAD is committed.

Sketch + CAD · 2–4 weeks · Ergonomics

Production-grade CAD from day one, then SLA, FDM and CNC parts built in-house. Looks-like and works-like models you can hold, test and put in front of buyers within days.

CAD · SLA / FDM / CNC · 1–3 weeks per loop

Hinges, latches, snap-fits, springs and drives. We engineer the moving parts that make a product feel precise, run the tolerance stack-ups, and cycle-test them until they survive real hands.

Mechanism design · Tolerances · Cycle tests

Draft angles, wall thickness, parting lines, material and process selection, and tooling-ready files — then we stand on the factory floor through samples and first article.

DFM review · Mould-ready CAD · BOM · T1 support

04 / Questions

Before we start.

The questions founders and product teams ask us most often.

What does an industrial design studio do?

We design physical products from the idea to mass production: research and concept sketches, 3D CAD, working prototypes, mechanisms such as hinges and latches, and the manufacturing files a factory needs to make the product.

How long does it take to design a new product?

Concept design usually takes 2–4 weeks, and each prototype loop 1–3 weeks. A complete programme from first sketch to production parts typically runs several months, depending on how complex the product and its mechanisms are.

How much does industrial design cost?

It depends on the scope. You can hire us for a single stage — a concept, a prototype or a DFM review — or for the whole journey. Send us a short brief and we will reply with a proposal.

Do you sign an NDA before seeing my idea?

Yes. We are happy to sign a non-disclosure agreement before you share any details of your product.

Can you help us manufacture the product?

Yes. Design for manufacturing is part of our core work: we prepare tooling-ready CAD, choose materials and processes with you, work with your factory or our manufacturing partners, and support you through samples and first-article parts.

Do you work with startups and individual founders?

Yes. We work with established brands, startups and individual founders. Many projects start with nothing more than a sketch or a competitor product you want to improve on.

Do you work with clients outside Israel?

Yes. The studio is in Tel Aviv and we work with clients worldwide, remotely and on site with manufacturers.

What should I send to get started?

A short description of the product, who it is for and where the project is today. Sketches, photos, reference products or an existing prototype all help, but none of them are required.
