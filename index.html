02 / Capabilities

Four moving parts, one assembly.

Most studios hand a drawing to a factory. We stay on the part — concept, prototype, mechanism and tooling are run by the same team, so a product reaches production with nothing lost in translation.

We start from the commercial brief, not the moodboard. Sketch cycles, orthographic layouts and 1:1 ergonomic mock-ups let us prove the proportions in the hand before any CAD is committed.

Sketch + CAD · 2–4 weeks · Ergonomics

Production-grade CAD from day one, then SLA, FDM and CNC parts built in-house. Looks-like and works-like models you can hold, test and put in front of buyers within days.

CAD · SLA / FDM / CNC · 1–3 weeks per loop

Hinges, latches, snap-fits, springs and drives. We engineer the moving parts that make a product feel precise, run the tolerance stack-ups, and cycle-test them until they survive real hands.

Mechanism design · Tolerances · Cycle tests

Draft angles, wall thickness, parting lines, material and process selection, and tooling-ready files — then we stand on the factory floor through samples and first article.

DFM review · Mould-ready CAD · BOM · T1 support

03 / Studio

We are the team that draws the part, tests the mechanism and stands on the floor at first article.

KLIPYDESIGN is an industrial design studio building consumer products for brands and founders. Our work starts where a business case meets a physical constraint — and ends when the moulded part comes out of the tool the way it left our screen.

That means complete idea-to-product support: form, mechanism, tolerances, tooling and the assembly documentation the factory actually reads. No handoffs between three vendors, no surprises at T1.
