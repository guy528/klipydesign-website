## The product

This low-profile paper towel holder features a non-skid weighted base and a super compact design. The adjustable side tab prevents the roll from unravelling and keeps everything in its place. Great for small spaces.

## Key features

- **Non-skid weighted base** that stays put on the counter.
- **Adjustable side tab** stops the roll from unravelling.
- **Super compact, low-profile design** for small kitchens.
- **Dishwasher safe and BPA free.**
