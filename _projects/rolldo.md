## The product

Rolldo cuts perfect circles in rolled-out dough and fondant. Its open, three-dimensional frame is made of continuous loops, and each one works as a cutting ring with a bevelled edge — so whichever way you pick it up, it's ready to cut. Press it down for single rounds or roll it across the dough to cut circle after circle in one smooth motion. Each cut is a clean 2-inch circle, ideal for cookies, tarts, ravioli, pierogi and more.

## Key features

- **Perfect 2-inch circles** in dough and fondant.
- **Every loop cuts** — easy to grip from any angle.
- **Press or roll** for fast, repeated cutting.
- **Made for cookies, tarts, ravioli, pierogi and more.**
