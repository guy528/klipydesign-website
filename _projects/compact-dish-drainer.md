## The product

This compact, stylishly designed rack is ideal for drying dishes when there is no room left in your main dish rack. Simply take it out of the cutlery drawer where it is stored when not in use, unfold it into its functional position in one easy step, and start placing your plates. Thanks to its compact size, it doesn't take up much space — even while in use.

## How it works

- **Stored flat** in the cutlery drawer, next to the knives and spoons, when it isn't needed.
- **Unfolds in one step** into its working position on the counter.
- **Holds plates upright** in a row of slots, so they drain and dry.
- **Small footprint** even when unfolded, so it fits beside the sink on a crowded counter.
