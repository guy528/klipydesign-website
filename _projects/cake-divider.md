## The product

Use this guide to divide your cake into a set number of even slices. Turn the dial to choose from 6, 8, 10 or 14 equal slices, then follow the guide with your knife. It looks great when every slice is exactly the same size — and there are no more arguments, or running out before everyone is served.

## Key features

- **Even slices every time** — the guide marks each cut for you.
- **Turn the dial** to choose 6, 8, 10 or 14 equal slices.
- **Fair portions**, so the cake goes round everyone.
- **Dishwasher safe and BPA free.**
