## The product

Peel ’n Mash puts two everyday kitchen tools into one. The masher's handle doubles as a home for a vegetable peeler — slide the peeler out to prepare your potatoes, then slide it back once you're done. The peeler has a sharp, swivelling stainless-steel blade and a comfortable grip, while the masher's slotted head makes quick work of potatoes, root vegetables and fruit. It's heat resistant, safe to use on non-stick cookware, and stands upright on the counter between uses.

## Key features

- **2-in-1:** a food masher with a vegetable peeler stored in its handle.
- **Sharp swivelling stainless-steel blade** with a comfortable grip.
- **Mashes potatoes, root vegetables and fruit.**
- **Heat resistant and safe on non-stick cookware.**
- **Stands upright** to keep the counter clean.
