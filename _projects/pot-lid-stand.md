## The product

The Pot Lid Stand provides a home for a wet, drippy pot lid and spoon. Its slim, modern wave shape holds a lid upright in one curve and cradles a cooking spoon in the next, keeping drips off the counter.

## Key features

- **Holds a pot lid upright** in its wave-shaped channel.
- **Rests a cooking spoon** in the same stand.
- **Slim, modern design** with a small footprint.
- **Dishwasher safe and BPA free.**
