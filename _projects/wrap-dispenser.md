## The product

The Wrap Dispenser gives foil, plastic wrap, wax paper and parchment a single, tidy home. Drop in a roll, close the hinged body and pull out exactly what you need — the built-in serrated edge cuts it cleanly in one quick motion. It takes rolls of up to 150 sq. ft. from your favourite brands, slips neatly into a drawer, and when a roll runs out you simply refill it. No more tangled wrap or torn boxes.

## Key features

- **One dispenser for every wrap** — foil, plastic wrap, wax paper and parchment.
- **Clean, quick-cutting edge** for a tidy tear every time.
- **Fits rolls up to 150 sq. ft.** from your favourite brands.
- **Stores neatly in a drawer.**
- **Easy to refill** — no more struggling with the box.
