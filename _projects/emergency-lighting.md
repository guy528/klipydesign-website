## The product

The Exit 8 LED series combines an illuminated exit sign and emergency lighting in a single wall-mounted fixture. It marks exits, escape routes and similar areas, while two adjustable LED spotlights light the way out. The housing is exceptionally slim and modern, so buildings that need emergency directional lighting can meet every relevant regulation without compromising on looks.

The series comes with a range of sign options to suit each site. Its electronics are built to the highest standards, in line with the international standard IEC 61347. If the power fails or an emergency occurs, the Exit 8 LED stays lit for more than two hours straight.

## Key features

- **Two in one:** illuminated exit sign plus emergency LED lighting.
- **Very low profile**, modern housing for wall mounting.
- **Range of sign options** to suit each building.
- **Electronics built to IEC 61347.**
- **More than two hours** of continuous light during a power failure.
