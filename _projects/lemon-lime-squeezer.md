## The product

The Lemon & Lime Squeezer is a soft silicone cup that you simply squeeze in your hand. Fresh juice flows out through the nozzle while the seeds stay inside, and your hands stay clean and dry. When you only need half, leave the rest in the cup, set it on its base and put it in the fridge — no cling film or extra wrapping needed. It also looks good on the dining table, so guests can add fresh lemon juice themselves.

## Key features

- **Clean, easy squeezing** for lemons and limes.
- **Hands stay clean and dry.**
- **Seeds stay inside** — only juice comes out.
- **Stores the leftover half** in the fridge on its base, with no wrapping needed.
- **Table-ready** for serving fresh lemon juice at meals.
