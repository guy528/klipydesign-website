## The product

Flexible Trivets bend to suit pots and serving dishes of any shape and size. A flexible metal core inside soft silicone holds whatever shape you give it — a ring for a round pot, a wave or two straight bars for a baking dish — and straightens out again to store flat in the cutlery drawer.

## Key features

- **Bends to any shape** to fit round pots, square dishes and everything in between.
- **Holds its shape** thanks to a flexible metal core inside silicone.
- **Stores flat** in a drawer when not in use.
- **Dishwasher safe and BPA free.**
