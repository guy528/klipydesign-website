## The product

Knife Blade Covers protect knife tips while covering the sharp blades, so knives can go straight into a drawer without dulling their edges or catching a hand. Each size has its own colour, which makes the right knife easy to find at a glance.

## Key features

- **Protects the tip and covers the blade** for safe drawer storage.
- **Three sizes** to fit 4-, 6- and 12-inch blades.
- **Colour-coded** — pink, green and grey.
- **Dishwasher safe and BPA free.**
