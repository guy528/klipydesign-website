## The product

No more wrestling with stubborn chip and cereal bags, or tearing them all the way down the side. ClipCut opens bags and closes them again with one simple tool. Place the top of the bag in the hidden cutter, press and slide across, and plastic or foil bags open safely and neatly. When you're done, fold the bag over and slip the clip side over the fold to keep everything fresh.

It works just as well on frozen food, coffee and candy bags, cheese packs, and even packaging and shipping envelopes. Small enough to carry anywhere, it's ideal for snacking on the go when there are no scissors around — picnics, barbecues, sports events, the beach, the pool and road trips.

## Key features

- **Cut and slide to open** plastic and foil bags with a hidden, safe cutter.
- **Clip to close** — keeps snacks and food fresh.
- **Many uses:** frozen food, coffee, candy, cheese, packaging and envelopes.
- **Pocket-sized** for picnics, the beach and road trips.
