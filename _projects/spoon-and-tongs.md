## The product

Spoon & Tongs is one utensil for cooking, scooping and serving. A spoon and a slotted pasta claw lock together at a pivot, so with one quick flip they work as tongs for tossing salad or lifting pasta. Pull them apart and you have a spoon and a server to use on their own. When you're done, they nest into a single slim piece that takes up no more drawer space than an ordinary spoon.

## Key features

- **Cook, scoop and serve** with one tool.
- **Flips from tongs to spoon** in a moment.
- **Separates** into a spoon and a pasta server for individual use.
- **Space saving** — nests into one slim piece for the drawer.
