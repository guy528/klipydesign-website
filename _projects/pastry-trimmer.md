## The product

The Pastry Trimmer puts three cutting edges into one compact wheel: a straight edge for clean cuts, a fluted edge for decorative borders, and a perforated edge that docks the dough. Just turn the dial to bring the edge you need into place. A clear guard keeps the wheels covered between uses. Use it for pies, pastry, ravioli, pierogi, fondant and more. When you're done, the sides come apart for easy cleaning.

## Key features

- **Three edges in one** — straight, fluted and perforated (dough docker).
- **Turn the dial** to switch between edges.
- **For pies, pastry, ravioli, pierogi, fondant and more.**
- **Sides come apart** for easy cleaning.
- **BPA free.**
