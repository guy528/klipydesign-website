## The product

The Herb Infuser brings the fresh flavour of herbs into soups and stocks while keeping the leaves themselves out of the bowl. Fill it with herbs, drop it into the pot and let the broth flow through the slots as it simmers. When the flavour is just right, lift it out — no floating bits to fish for, and nothing left behind.

## Key features

- **Fresh herb flavour** for soups, stocks and broths.
- **No floating bits** — the slotted cage keeps every leaf inside.
- **Quick to fill** and even quicker to clean.
- **Dishwasher safe and BPA free.**
