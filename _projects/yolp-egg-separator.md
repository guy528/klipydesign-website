## The product

Yolp makes separating eggs quick and tidy. Its wave-shaped body sits securely on the rim of a bowl, so both hands are free to crack the egg straight into it. The white slips through the slots into the bowl below, while the curved centre cradles the yolk. Then lift Yolp by its end and tip the yolk gently into a second bowl. No passing the yolk from shell to shell, and no broken yolks in your whites.

## Key features

- **Rests on any bowl rim**, leaving your hands free.
- **Slots let the egg white drain** straight into the bowl.
- **Curved centre holds the yolk** safely in place.
- **Easy yolk transfer** to a second bowl.
- **Smooth and easy to clean.**
