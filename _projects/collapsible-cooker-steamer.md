## The product

Easily prepare a variety of different foods in the same pot with the Collapsible Cooker. This 1-quart basket is for boiling, steaming and straining. Up to three baskets fit in a 4-quart pot at once, so pasta, potatoes and vegetables can cook side by side and come out separately.

## Key features

- **Boil, steam and strain** in one 1-quart basket.
- **Three in one pot** — up to three baskets fit in a 4-quart pot at the same time.
- **Easy-lift handle** that stays comfortable to hold, and a **hook** for hanging on the pot rim.
- **Collapses flat** for compact storage when not in use.
- **Dishwasher safe and BPA free.**
